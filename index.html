CDL Program

Become a certified
school bus driver.

1

Obtain Your Chauffeur License

Upgrade your standard Michigan driver's license with a 20-question chauffeur knowledge test at the Secretary of State office.

2

Complete CLP Permit Exams

Pass the Commercial Learners Permit exams: General Knowledge, Air Brakes, Passenger Transport, and School Bus endorsements — all can be taken same day.

3

MDE Beginning Driver Course

Complete the Michigan Department of Education's 3–4 day classroom certification course, available monthly through local ISDs.

4

Behind-the-Wheel Training

14–21 days of hands-on training with our certified instructors before your state road test — pre-trip inspection, backing maneuvers, and on-road skills.

5

State CDL Road Test

Schedule with a certified state examiner. Pass pre-trip, skills, and road sections to earn your Commercial Driver's License.
